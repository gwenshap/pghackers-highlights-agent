1. **Reduce timing overhead of EXPLAIN ANALYZE using rdtsc?** — Andres Freund identifies that a performance regression in pg_test_timing comes from overflow logic triggering too early with CLOCK_MONOTONIC (after just 3 days uptime), and suggests keeping values in instr_time domain rather than converting to nanoseconds prematurely.
